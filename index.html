Major projects

Magento-B2B

A cut-down ERP that uses Magento as the web shopping cart and Xero as the accounting software. It synchronises products, orders and customers with Magento, and all accounting transactions with Xero.

Modules include Sales, CRM, Logistics, Workshop and Reporting.

GitHubView it live

EBMV

A Chinese e-resources platform for libraries. Library users can read and download e-magazines, e-newspapers and ebooks with their library card and PIN.

It connects libraries to the e-resource suppliers and checks users against the library's own system, so the platform holds no user information.

GitHubView it live

Easy Bookkeeping

A small programme for daily bookkeeping that does the analysis, budgeting and property management automatically.

You can attach documents such as invoices, images and PDFs to each transaction. Modules include Bookkeeping, Property Management and Reporting.

GitHubView it live

Properta

A small system for property managers to view, track and maintain properties.

It notifies tenants and owners automatically, tells the manager which properties are due for inspection, and sends inspection reports to owners once they're done.
